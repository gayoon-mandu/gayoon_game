정답은 없습니다. 다만, 당신의 선택과 그것들이 만든 결과만이 남습니다.

IRENE

아이린 레드필드 (Irene Redfield)

Name: Irene Redfield

Husband: Dr. Brian Redfield

Occupation: Black upper-middle class housewife in Harlem

Personality: Cautious, Responsible, Realistic

Trait: Prioritizes stability and is committed to protecting her family.

Though she strives to maintain her peaceful daily life, cracks begin to form with Clare's sudden appearance.

CLARE

클레어 벨루 (Clare Bellew)

Name: Clare Bellew

Husband: John Bellew

Occupation: Upper-class lady in white society

Personality: Bold, Passionate, Charming

Trait: Lives a life of 'passing', completely hiding her heritage.

Despite the risks, reconnecting with her childhood friend Irene draws her back to the warmth of Harlem and the life she thought she had left behind.
